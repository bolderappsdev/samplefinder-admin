1. Summary

The SampleFinder app needs to send a one-time code by text message so new users can confirm their phone number. Before any US business can send texts, the carriers require the messaging campaign to be registered and approved. Our registration was reviewed and rejected.

The good news: your business identity (the Brand) is already approved. Only the campaign registration was rejected, and it can be corrected and resubmitted at no extra cost.

There are two pieces of work, and they must happen in this order:

PartWhat needs to happenWho does it
Part A Correct the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy pages on samplefinder.com Whoever maintains the website, with legal sign-off
Part B Update and resubmit the campaign in the Twilio Console Whoever has the Twilio account login
Important: Do not start Part B until Part A is published and live on the website. The reviewers open your policy pages and read them. If the pages still contradict each other when they look, the campaign will be rejected a second time and you will wait through another full review cycle.

2. Why the campaign was rejected

Twilio returned error code 30908. That code means the reviewer could not accept the privacy policy attached to the registration. In our case there are two specific problems.

Problem 1: The Terms and the Privacy Policy disagree with each other

The two pages currently describe the use of customer phone numbers differently. When a reviewer finds two published documents that say different things, they cannot determine which one governs, so the registration is rejected. Twilio lists this explicitly as a cause: conflicting policies create ambiguity about which one applies.

This is the main blocker. It is a wording problem on the website, not a problem with the app.

Problem 2: The registration was submitted before the URL requirement existed

Our campaign was created on 23 June 2026. On 30 June 2026, one week later, the carriers made the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links mandatory fields on every campaign. Our submission predates that change, so it never carried those links. They must be added now.

3. Part A: Update the website legal pages

These are the two pages under review. Both must be publicly reachable with no login and no password.

  • https://samplefinder.com/terms-and-conditions/
  • https://samplefinder.com/privacy-policy/

Step A1: Remove the contradiction

Read both pages side by side and make every statement about phone numbers, text messages and consent agree. Where they differ today, the Privacy Policy wording is the version to keep, because it is already close to what the carriers expect. A drafted amendment for the Terms page has been prepared and supplied separately alongside this document.

Step A2: Confirm the Privacy Policy contains all four required statements

Carriers require these four points to appear in the Privacy Policy. Check each one and add anything missing.

  • Non-sharing statement. The policy must say clearly that mobile phone numbers and text message consent are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes. This exact point is the single most common reason campaigns fail.
  • What data is collected and why. A plain explanation of the customer information collected and how it is used.
  • Message frequency. For SampleFinder this is one message per verification request.
  • Rate disclosure. The sentence "Message and data rates may apply" must appear.

If the non-sharing statement is missing, this wording is acceptable and can be pasted directly into the Privacy Policy:

Suggested wording for the Privacy Policy

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging consent are collected solely to verify your account and are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency is one message per verification request. Message and data rates may apply.

Step A3: Verify the pages before moving on

  • Open both links in a private or incognito browser window. They must load without any login.
  • Confirm there is only one privacy policy published on the site. If an older copy exists at a different address, remove it or redirect it.
  • Confirm both pages load correctly on a mobile phone.
  • Confirm the two pages no longer contradict each other on phone numbers or messaging.
Only continue to Part B once every box above is ticked. The reviewer will visit these pages during the review.

4. Part B: Update the campaign in Twilio

You will need the login for the Twilio account that owns the SampleFinder messaging service.

Edit the existing campaign. Do not delete it and create a new one. Twilio charges the vetting fee only once per campaign, and editing keeps that history. Deleting and recreating starts a fresh registration and can be charged again.
  1. Open https://console.twilio.com in your browser and sign in.
  2. If your login has access to more than one Twilio account or project, use the account selector at the top of the page and choose the account used by SampleFinder. Getting this wrong is easy and everything after it will look empty.
  3. In the left hand menu, click Messaging.
  4. Under Messaging, click Regulatory Compliance.
  5. Click Campaigns. The full navigation path is Messaging > Regulatory Compliance > Campaigns.
  6. Find the campaign with the ID QE2c6890da8086d771620e9b13fadeba0b. Its status will read FAILED. Click the campaign name or the ID to open it.
  7. On the Campaign Details page, read the rejection reason shown near the top so you can confirm it still says the privacy policy issue. Then click the blue Edit Campaign link.
  8. A form opens. Find the field labelled Message Flow, sometimes shown as Call to Action / Message Flow or How do end users consent to receive messages. Delete everything currently in that field.
  9. Copy the Message Flow text from section 6 of this document and paste it into that field. Paste it exactly as written, including both web links. Do not shorten it.
  10. Scroll to the Sample Messages section. Confirm that at least two sample messages are present and that they match section 6 of this document. Replace or add them if they do not match.
  11. Check the consent and opt-in checkboxes on the form. Confirm that the options describing subscriber opt-in, help message and opt-out are all ticked, since the SampleFinder app provides all three.
  12. Review the whole form once more, then click Update to submit. The status will change to pending while the campaign is reviewed again.
If you cannot find an Edit Campaign link, it usually means the campaign was registered under a different Twilio account, or the use case selected at creation cannot be edited. Contact the development team before deleting anything.

5. Part C: After you submit

What to expect

  • Resubmitting starts a completely new review by the carrier registry. It is not a quick recheck.
  • Reviews commonly take a few business days and can take up to about three weeks.
  • There is no additional vetting fee for resubmitting the same campaign, and there is no limit on how many times a campaign may be resubmitted.

How to check the status

Return to Messaging > Regulatory Compliance > Campaigns at any time and look at the status column. You are waiting for it to change from pending to VERIFIED or APPROVED.

Please tell the development team as soon as the status changes. Text message verification stays switched off in the live app until it is approved, so nothing changes for customers before then.

If it is rejected again

Open the campaign and read the new rejection code, then send the development team a screenshot of the Campaign Details page showing the code and the reason. A different code means a different problem, and each one has its own fix. Do not resubmit an unchanged campaign, because it will simply be rejected again.

7. Common questions

Why can we not just start sending texts?

US mobile carriers block unregistered business text traffic. Without an approved campaign, verification codes are either silently dropped or rejected outright, so customers would never receive them.

Does this affect the live app today?

No. Phone verification is switched off in the released app and stays off until the campaign is approved. Customers are unaffected in the meantime.

Do we need to change the app?

No. The app already collects consent correctly, with an unchecked box, the required disclosure wording, and working HELP and STOP handling. All remaining work is on the website and in the Twilio Console.

Can we speed this up?

The review time is controlled by the carrier registry and cannot be expedited. The only real way to save time is to get Part A completely right before submitting, so a second rejection is avoided.
